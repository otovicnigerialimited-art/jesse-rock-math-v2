Full Application Features & Capabilities

1. Live Multiplayer Battle Arena

An active, real-time classroom hub where students join shared game lobbies instantly. Powered by an advanced real-time Firebase backend, classmates compete side-by-side to solve math problems. The arena includes dynamic matchmaking, live spectator scores, and competitive multiplayer countdowns that keep students fully engaged during classroom math drills. The real-time mechanics synchronize score updates instantly using Firestore listeners, allowing children to see their friends' progress, trigger live animations, and share healthy, non-toxic emoji reactions.

2. Real-Time Adaptive Learning Drills (Solo Mode)

An intelligent mathematical algorithm handles solo play. As a child enters correct answers and builds their streak, the math questions dynamically increase in complexity (e.g., larger digits, multi-operator equations, and challenging fractions). When an error occurs, the difficulty smoothly recalibrates downwards to protect the learner's confidence and foster growth mindset resilience. This adaptive progression system ensures that students are always challenged at their exact skill level, preventing math anxiety while encouraging cognitive development.

3. Interactive Teacher Classroom Hub & Live Chalkboard

Teachers can create customized Class Codes instantly. Once students join with the code, the teacher acts as the moderator on a digital chalkboard, broadcast-controlling the math subject (multiplication, decimals, algebra, etc.), approving student entries, publishing daily math vocabulary words, and setting assignments. This transforms typical math sessions into active, synchronized multiplayer activities. The live chalkboard is reactive, updating instantly on student devices whenever the teacher edits equations or broadcasts new terms.

4. Rockstar Avatar Shop & Rewards Center

Students earn Math Rock Tokens and Experience Points (XP) solely through mathematical accuracy and persistent practice. These tokens are 100% free with absolutely zero microtransactions. In the Rockstar Shop, students redeem tokens for cosmetic profile upgrades, exclusive user badges (e.g., "Math Legend", "Fractions Champion"), and unlockable rock instruments (electric guitars, drums, synths) to personalize their avatars. This visual-reward mechanism builds healthy extrinsic motivation, inspiring children to practice more math to acquire rare shop items.
